Who Built This

Alex Chiriac

I spent 12 years in the pool. Club, county, nationals. My coach gave us dryland sheets and they had structure. He adjusted reps and load depending on the season. But it was still one sheet for the whole team. Same exercises, same order, every time. I always felt like it could be sharper. More personalised. More adapted to what I actually had access to. So I built it.
