Pre-Season — You're weeks out from competition. Heavy compound lifts, explosive plyometrics, maximum power. Build the engine.

In-Season — Training and racing regularly. Maintain strength without creating fatigue. Leave the gym activated, not wrecked.

Taper — Race week. Light mobility, activation, core only. No fatigue. The finisher auto-disables.

Off-Season — No meets. Higher volume, more muscle. This is where the V-taper comes from.

Blue tags = primary muscles. The main targets the exercise is designed to work.

Grey tags = secondary muscles. These assist during the movement but aren't the main focus.
